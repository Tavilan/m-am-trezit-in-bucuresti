STYLE GUIDE for the game "M-am trezit în București" (I Woke Up in Bucharest), applies to every image:
- Detailed, atmospheric PIXEL ART illustration with crisp, clean pixel clusters, as if drawn at about 640x360 and shown at 3x. Rich, unlimited colour palette; no dithering noise.
- REALISM FIRST: real Romanian places, objects, clothing and proportions, faithful to real life. Nothing generic or fantasy.
- LEGIBILITY: every object and person must be instantly recognisable at a glance: clear silhouettes, strong value contrast against the background, important things large and in the foreground or midground. No tiny clutter that reads as noise.
- Lighting: beautiful, cinematic, warm and cold light sources (bulbs, sodium lamps, neon, moonlight) over scenes that are shabby, comic or grim.
- Tone: affectionate satire of Romanian everyday life. Mock situations, behaviours and institutions, never ethnic groups, poor people or the homeless.
- NO TEXT, letters, numbers, logos or watermarks anywhere in the image unless the prompt explicitly asks for specific text. Signs, posters and screens must be left BLANK; the game adds text later.
- No real brands, no real people, no famous characters.
- PEOPLE: every character has their own face and body type, exactly as the prompt describes; never reuse another character's face.
